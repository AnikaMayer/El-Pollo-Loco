Class: BabyChicken

BabyChicken()

Represents a baby chicken enemy that walks randomly left and right across the map. Plays a death sound and switches to a dead animation when killed.

Constructor

new BabyChicken()

Creates a new BabyChicken at a random position with a random speed and direction, and starts its movement, animation and sound intervals.
